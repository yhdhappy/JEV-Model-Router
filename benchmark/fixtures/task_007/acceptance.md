# Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `REVIEW.md` exists and is the only changed path.
- [ ] Every broad `except Exception` in `router.py` and `fallback.py` is
      accounted for with an exact function and line reference.
- [ ] The review distinguishes intentional normalization from dangerous
      masking and explains secret-safety implications.
- [ ] At least two concrete, reproducible probes and a smallest remediation
      plan are included.
- [ ] The document contains analysis only: no fabricated score, result,
      provider model, price, credential, or Pilot decision.
